GridFusion Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

GridFusion is a puzzle game developed by Jack Bouton.

Information We Collect

GridFusion does not collect, store, or share personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photographs, audio, or other personal information.

Data Sharing

GridFusion does not share user information with third parties.

Analytics and Advertising

GridFusion does not use advertising services or analytics services to collect information about users.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because GridFusion does not collect or maintain personal information, there is no personal user data retained by the developer and no account or personal-data deletion process is required.

Children's Privacy

GridFusion does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of GridFusion change, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or GridFusion's privacy practices, contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play listing.
